      Instapress

      Turns your own Instagram posts into WordPress drafts: it loads your posts, lets you edit the caption (title, text, categories, image, video, slug and SEO fields) and creates the result as a draft on your site through the WordPress REST API. Nothing is published until you review the draft in WordPress.

      Your Instagram token and your WordPress application password are kept in the operating system's credential store.
